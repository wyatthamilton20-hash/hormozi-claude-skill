## Frameworks
### Content unit
1. Hook: win attention with topic, headline and format.
2. Retain: embed open questions with lists, steps and stories.
3. Reward: satisfy the reason they came.
Use when: making any content; chain units for longer pieces. (5268c7623b@00:07:04)

### Five topic sources
1. Far past. 2. Recent past. 3. Present. 4. Trending. 5. Manufactured.
Use when: you need content topics. (5268c7623b@00:09:18)

### Headline news components
Use at least two: recency, relevancy, celebrity, proximity, conflict, unusual, ongoing.
Use when: writing any headline. (5268c7623b@00:13:28)

### Width then depth
1. Get on every relevant platform early.
2. Maximize all platforms together.
Use when: content can be repurposed across platforms. (5268c7623b@00:32:39)

## Warnings
- Free content is harder to personalize -> fewer people respond than to a personal message. Applies to: all. (5268c7623b@00:06:29)
- Content that stands out gets copied by competitors -> keep innovating. Applies to: all. (5268c7623b@00:06:40)
- Content that does not match its hook -> viewers stop watching and sharing. Applies to: all. (5268c7623b@00:20:00)
- Inside jokes -> newcomers feel unwelcome and growth slows. Applies to: all. (5268c7623b@00:22:25)
- Asking too often -> goodwill and audience shrink. Applies to: long-form with integrated asks. (5268c7623b@00:27:51)
- Lying to customers -> a bad reputation sticks for life. Applies to: all. (e2d07b0142@00:09:45)
