## Frameworks
### Core four
1. Warm one-to-one (reach-outs). 2. Warm one-to-many (content). 3. Cold one-to-one (outreach). 4. Cold one-to-many (paid ads). Use when: choosing how to get engaged leads. (0e627cd524@00:02:10)

### Rule of 100
1. 100 warm reach-outs per day. 2. 100 minutes of content per day. 3. 100 cold reach-outs per day. 4. 100 minutes of paid ads per day. Use when: starting any core-four activity. (eb47202949@00:03:49)

### More, better, new
1. More: raise volume. 2. Better: improve the constraint step. 3. New: placements, then platforms, then activities. Use when: an activity gives too few leads. (eb47202949@00:02:44)

### Four lead levers
1. More leads. 2. Better leads. 3. Cheaper leads. 4. Reliable leads. Use when: improving lead flow. (fa5ba3d2a4@00:24:40)

### Five lead stages
1. Uncontacted. 2. Contacted. 3. Engaged. 4. Qualified. 5. Sold. Use when: tracking where leads stall. (71cc0aa386@00:07:46)

### Friction levers
1. More qualification. 2. More information requested. 3. More steps. 4. Forced consumption. 5. Longer ads. Use when: volume is high and quality is low. (cb6c7243ae@00:04:21)

### Referral system
1. Build goodwill with six value steps: call outs, dream outcome, likelihood, time delay, effort, call to action. 2. Ask with seven methods: one-sided reward, two-sided reward, ask at purchase, negotiation chip, events, ongoing programs, unlockable bonuses. Use when: customers do not refer. (762576cd50@00:14:28) (762576cd50@00:25:55)

### Affiliate offer and payout
1. Offer: more money than their current offer, dream outcome, likelihood, low effort, short delay. 2. Pay 25% of max CAC after signup and purchase, 50% after activation, 100% after sustained performance. Use when: building an affiliate program. (aa1e69e7d7@00:08:37) (aa1e69e7d7@00:12:57)

### Landing page layouts
1. Headline, sub-headline, opt-in. 2. Add an image. 3. Add benefit bullets below the fold. Use when: collecting contact details from ads. (4531ed2ef6@00:31:10)
