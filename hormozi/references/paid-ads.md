## Top rules

- IF you have not run an ad yet THEN set up return tracking before you spend (4531ed2ef6@00:35:51)

## Decision tree by stage
### S0
- Primary: Do not open with paid ads; run warm outreach, content and cold outreach first, because they build the skill and fund ads (4531ed2ef6@00:48:57)
- IF no cold conversion mechanism is proven THEN keep ads off the table as the first bet (786a65ca80@01:50:17)
- Do not: buy cold traffic before any offer converts cold prospects (786a65ca80@01:50:17)

### S1
- Primary: Spend only an amount you can lose each month, and expect to lose it while you learn (4531ed2ef6@00:47:57)
- IF you have never run an ad THEN place one real ad yourself, for about $100 (4531ed2ef6@00:47:21)
- IF you test a new ad THEN use the 2x / 1x test budget rule (4531ed2ef6@00:37:37)
- IF you sell locally THEN call out the area plus the type of person, and add few filters (4531ed2ef6@00:13:53)
- Do not: judge a channel on a tiny test; 300 flyers cannot be read (fcab34dee8@00:07:44)

### S2
- Primary: Once an ad is profitable in a small audience, widen it from puddle to pond to lake to ocean (4531ed2ef6@00:05:28)
- IF ads break even or better and you have a sales goal THEN set the budget from customers wanted, plus 20% (4531ed2ef6@00:39:00)
- IF ads stop converting THEN run the same ad with a new video or image (0841ea3225@00:10:48)
- IF a free post performs well THEN run it as a paid ad (4531ed2ef6@00:46:20)
- IF results plateau THEN write about 10 new ads and 30 call-outs each week (4531ed2ef6@00:18:29)
- Do not: conclude that ads cannot scale; usually the ad is mediocre (eb47202949@00:04:57)

### S3
- Primary: No direct rule in the books.
- IF you can profitably pay more per customer than rivals THEN raise ad spend until you claim the niche (1fce7b6688@00:01:36)
- IF ad returns sit near 2:1 THEN fix the offer and money model so customers fund growth (08c431487f@00:05:14)
- IF the goal is volume or market share THEN do not rely on a straight premium offer (9662e6e5c3@00:08:00)

### S4
- Primary: No direct rule in the books.
- IF you spend on ads every month THEN reserve 1%, 5% or 10% of the budget for tests with no expected return (fcab34dee8@00:03:48)
- IF you calculate CAC THEN include media buyer payroll, media, software and commissions (4a2ac84b02@00:02:35)

### S5
- Primary: No direct rule in the books.
- IF you already outspend the niche THEN keep raising spend; newcomers cannot afford to buy ads there (1fce7b6688@00:01:36)

## Frameworks
### Needles in the haystack
1. Pick a platform where your ideal audience is.
2. Target within it.
3. Craft an ad that repels everyone else.
4. Tell the rest what to do next.
Use when: planning any paid campaign. (4531ed2ef6@00:05:04)

### Four problems of paid ads
1. Choose the platform: used it as a consumer, can target the audience, know the formats, can afford the minimum spend.
2. Get the right audience to see the ad.
3. Make the best ad for them.
4. Get permission to contact them.
Use when: choosing and setting up a platform. (4531ed2ef6@00:06:13) (4531ed2ef6@00:06:46)

### Ad structure
1. Call-out: gets attention.
2. Value: creates interest.
3. Call to action: tells people what to do next.
Use when: writing any ad. (4531ed2ef6@00:10:53)

### What, who, when (value in the ad)
1. What: the eight value elements: dream outcome vs nightmare, perceived likelihood vs risk, time delay vs speed, effort and sacrifice vs ease.
2. Who: status the customer gains, and how spouse, boss and rivals treat them.
3. When: past, present and future consequences of buying or not, from their view and from people they know.
Use when: writing the value part of an ad. (4531ed2ef6@00:19:39) (4531ed2ef6@00:20:35)

## Warnings
- Blaming the channel for a mediocre ad -> you stop before ads scale. Applies to: all. (eb47202949@00:04:57)
- Too little volume -> ads seem not to work. Applies to: all. (fcab34dee8@00:08:57)
- A quantified result plus a duration where banned -> it implies a guarantee and breaks platform rules. Applies to: all. (0841ea3225@00:05:32)
- Accepting 2:1 returns -> you miss customer-financed growth. Applies to: all, C-ECON. (08c431487f@00:05:14)
- A straight premium offer for volume -> needs many more eyeballs; least efficient for market share. Applies to: all, C-ECON. (9662e6e5c3@00:08:00)
