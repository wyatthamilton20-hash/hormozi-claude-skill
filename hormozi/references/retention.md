## Frameworks
### Monthly friction fix
1. Survey customers for the most common problem.
2. Fix it and test the fix with struggling customers.
3. Roll it out if it works, then take the next problem.
Use when: you want more goodwill and referrals. (762576cd50@00:22:54)

### Continuity bonus design
1. Set the when: a delay or a milestone.
2. Set the what: one-time, variable, or lifetime upgrade.
3. If unsure, give more or better, and make it faster, easier or risk-free.
Use when: you build a continuity offer. (fdcf529421@00:04:15) (fdcf529421@00:05:11)

### Cancellation save sequence
1. Show how to cancel. (47db2b3f88@00:22:59)
2. Exit interview; let them vent. (47db2b3f88@00:23:27)
3. Remind them of the discount they lose. (cd9e0b7e12@00:06:49)
4. Offer a feature downsell or rollover upsell. (b2852988cd@00:35:20) (9baf8df5b6@00:37:07)
Use when: a customer asks to cancel.

## Warnings
- Selling mainly on the guarantee -> buyers who do not do the work. Applies to: all. (9ef09b14d9@00:14:53)
- Fees used to hold customers to a bad product -> the product problem stays. Applies to: continuity. (47db2b3f88@00:29:37)
- No clear way to cancel -> complaints outside the business. Applies to: all. (47db2b3f88@00:22:59)
- Bad free content -> customers like the paid product less. Applies to: all. (5268c7623b@00:38:28)
- No upsell purchase after a free front end -> customer is unlikely to stay. Applies to: free or trial offers. (0a1fc80b7d@00:07:42)
