## Frameworks
### Menu upsell
1. Unsell what they do not need. 2. Prescribe what they do need. 3. Ask A or B. 4. Ask to use the card on file. Use when: several offers exist, in-person sales. (63b42b8759@00:23:06) (9baf8df5b6@00:17:09)

### Four guarantee types
1. Unconditional. 2. Conditional. 3. Anti-guarantee (all sales final, only with a credible reason; suits consumables). 4. Implied (performance-based). Use when: designing risk reversal. (9ef09b14d9@00:13:37) (9ef09b14d9@00:17:17)

### Launch: whisper, tease, shout
1. Whisper every 4-6 weeks until 60 days out, then every 2-3 weeks until 30 days out. 2. Tease weekly until 14 days out, then twice weekly until 3 days out. 3. Shout twice a day from 3 days out, every few hours on launch day, every 30 minutes in the last 2 hours. Use when: any launch. (aa1e69e7d7@00:17:23) (aa1e69e7d7@00:19:49)

### Presentation content
1. Clarify the problem. 2. Show life after the fix. 3. Show how fast and easy it is. 4. Cite similar buyers who succeeded. 5. Resolve common concerns. Use when: a presentation comes before the offer. (db154a35b9@00:02:24)

### Free presentation formats
1. Free dinner: present, lower offer covers dinner, book appointments for the higher ticket. 2. Five-day challenge: four live sessions Monday-Thursday, offer Friday; one core belief per day. 3. Three-day virtual summit: offer help at the end of day two and on day three. Use when: complex, high-ticket or cold-traffic offers. (db154a35b9@00:04:30) (db154a35b9@00:03:50)

### Sales video for low show rate
1. Promise, plan, proof. 2. Four beliefs, each with why it is wrong, what is right, and proof. Use when: show rate is low because the video lacks proof. (786a65ca80@01:19:42)
