## Top rules

## Frameworks
### Value equation
1. Value = (dream outcome x perceived likelihood) / (time delay x effort and sacrifice).
2. Raise the top; push the bottom toward zero.
Use when: building or comparing any offer. (62ae23e9bc@00:03:48)

### Grand Slam offer build
1. Dream outcome. 2. List problems. 3. Turn problems into solutions. 4. List delivery options (delivery cube: attention, customer effort, medium, format, reply speed). 5. Trim high-cost low-value items, stack the rest.
Use when: making an offer from scratch. (2762e31770@00:29:16) (2762e31770@00:22:46)

### Offer enhancers
1. Scarcity (seats, bonuses, never-again items). 2. Urgency. 3. Bonuses. 4. Guarantees. 5. Naming.
Use when: the product is fixed and demand must rise. (1053bd922a@00:10:43) (1053bd922a@00:16:42)

### MAGIC naming
1. Magnet (reason why). 2. Avatar. 3. Goal. 4. Interval. 5. Container. Use 3-5 parts.
Use when: naming offers, bonuses and sub-items. (0841ea3225@00:02:22)

### Fatigue change order
1. Ad creative. 2. Body copy. 3. Headline or wrapper. 4. Duration. 5. Free or discount enhancer. 6. Monetization structure.
Use when: lead flow drops on a working offer. (0841ea3225@00:10:00)

### Lead magnet
1. Type: reveal a problem, sample or trial, or one step of a process. 2. Delivery: software, information, service or physical. 3. Seven steps: problem and audience, solution, delivery, name test, easy to consume, very good, easy to ask for more.
Use when: building a free front end. (1195bc3c24@00:11:38) (1195bc3c24@00:09:07)

### Avatar process
1. Survey customers with a benefit for finishing. 2. Sort top 20%. 3. Find 3-5 shared qualifiers. 4. Rewrite ads and sales process for them.
Use when: you have customers and want better leads. (2f3dbafa8b@00:12:03)

### Trial with penalty
1. Offer last, after a clear no. 2. Card on file. 3. Ask about long-term stay. 4. Explain fees after the card. 5. Required check-ins.
Use when: recurring product needs customer effort. (b2852988cd@00:19:31)

## Warnings
- Repeating the same promotion too soon -> each repeat sells fewer until none sell. Applies to: all. (1053bd922a@00:09:05)
- Fake deadlines -> lost credibility. Applies to: all. (1053bd922a@00:27:53)
- Discounting the main offer to close -> buyers learn prices are negotiable. Applies to: all, C-CONV. (9ef09b14d9@00:02:33)
- Always discounting the core -> buyers only buy on discount. Applies to: all. (7f741b1812@00:10:42)
- Discounting a service buyers do not understand -> offer fails. Applies to: online-service/agency. (7f741b1812@00:03:02)
- A strong offer to the wrong audience -> it fails. Applies to: S0-S1. (7593d0b5ef@00:16:00)
- Selling to anyone who can pay -> high churn and acquisition cost, low satisfaction. Applies to: all. (2f3dbafa8b@00:09:11)
- Bigger and bigger claims -> easy to copy, not unique. Applies to: S0-S1. (62ae23e9bc@00:05:16)
- Inflated bonus values -> no anchor, lost trust. Applies to: all. (47db2b3f88@00:08:31)
- Treating the anchor as fake -> the buyer ignores it. Applies to: all. (9baf8df5b6@00:28:31)
- Fake lifetime discount -> misleading. Applies to: all. (cd9e0b7e12@00:02:40)
- Freemium without capital or known numbers -> failure. Applies to: saas/software. (65f6e81dc4@00:00:21) (65f6e81dc4@00:04:09)
