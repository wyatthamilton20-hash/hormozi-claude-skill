## Decision tree by stage
### S0
- Primary: Do not build a full money model. Start at stage one: choose one attraction offer that turns strangers into customers and covers costs. (fa0c7f3536@00:03:45) (fa0c7f3536@00:07:30)
- IF bootstrapped with little capital THEN avoid CFA level one; acquire customers at a profit from the first sale. (08c431487f@00:00:36) (fa0c7f3536@00:09:26)
- IF copy or sales process is not proven THEN use a free or discount offer; the offer carries weak copy. (9662e6e5c3@00:06:59)
- Do not: lead with a premium offer when starting out or when cash is tight. (9662e6e5c3@00:02:57)

### S1
- Primary: With low volume or high CAC, wrap the premium offer in a free or discount offer to get flow, then monetize the flow. (9662e6e5c3@00:09:35) (1b03f53392@00:04:42)
- IF the attraction offer is new THEN advertise it and check that leads become customers; finding the right one can take up to a year. (fa0c7f3536@00:07:51)
- IF early stage, before tests of prepaid and bulk offers THEN offer bonuses standalone and continuity month to month. (47db2b3f88@00:13:12)
- IF you need money fast THEN give something away; free often produces the fastest money. (1b03f53392@00:05:36)
- Do not: build the business on discount cash; it only offsets acquisition cost. (7f741b1812@00:04:27)

### S2
- Primary: If one product has reliable customers, add an upsell before revenue plateaus. (fa0c7f3536@00:00:58)
- IF a single upsell stops converting THEN offer a menu of service levels, then feature downsells. (fa0c7f3536@00:02:09)
- IF one channel has a lower CAC THEN do more of that channel. (4a2ac84b02@00:03:32)
- IF payback is longer than 30 days THEN add an immediate upsell to recover CAC in the first 30 days. (4531ed2ef6@00:44:31)
- Do not: implement the whole money model at once. (fa0c7f3536@00:09:42)

### S3

- IF LTGP:CAC is already about 20:1 THEN leave the core model alone and solve only the remaining constraint. (786a65ca80@00:45:41)

## Frameworks
### Money model build stages
1. Get customers reliably.
2. Make them pay for themselves.
3. Make them pay for other customers.
4. Maximize lifetime value.
5. Spend ad dollars to scale.
Use when: planning the model as the business grows; each stage must pay for the next. (fa0c7f3536@00:04:00)

### Customer-financed acquisition (CFA) levels
1. Level one: 30-day gross profit below CAC; you wait to recover the gap.
2. Level two: 30-day gross profit equals CAC; the credit limit caps growth.
3. Level three: gross profit above 2x CAC; put the extra into more customers.
Use when: judging if customers can finance growth. (08c431487f@00:02:25) (08c431487f@00:01:16)

### Four offer types
1. Attraction: free or discount to get customers.
2. Upsell: more, better or new; classic, menu, anchor, rollover.
3. Downsell: payment plan, trial with penalty, feature downsell.
4. Continuity: bonus, discount, waived fee.
Use when: choosing what to sell next. (8f7d70af7e@00:01:03) (9baf8df5b6@00:02:12)

### Payment-plan downsell ladder
1. Full price, paid in full.
2. Third-party financing, then credit card, then layaway.
3. Half now, half on next paycheck.
4. Ask desire on a 1-10 scale.
5. If 8 or above: thirds over two paychecks or months.
6. Spread evenly over the service.
Stop when they buy. Use when: the customer cannot pay in full. (b2852988cd@00:06:49) (b2852988cd@00:10:06)

### Offer check
1. Right stage. 2. Right problem. 3. Right way. 4. Right time.
Use when: adding any new offer. (3d6996b5df@00:12:03)

### One-time fee
1. Pick the fee name. 2. Pick the price. 3. Pick the reason. 4. Charge, discount or waive it.
Use when: any recurring service or fixed-length program. (08f4105dbf@00:04:47)

### Referral growth equation
Monthly percent of clients referred minus monthly percent churned equals monthly compounding growth. Use when: planning referral-driven growth. (762576cd50@00:11:42)

## Warnings
- Acquisition cost above the profit a customer returns -> the business loses money on each new customer. Applies to: all. (e2d07b0142@00:03:31)
- Slow payback without cash reserves -> cut ads, loans, equity sales, then loss of the business. Applies to: businesses without large reserves. (e2d07b0142@00:03:44) (e2d07b0142@00:04:29)
- Loans or investors used too early -> problems later. Applies to: all. (d734fb1d90@00:01:05)
- Counting only ad spend or treating content leads as free -> missing profit at month end. Applies to: all. (4a2ac84b02@00:00:25)
- Payment plans where customers cancel before you profit -> lost money. Applies to: all. (b2852988cd@00:05:39)
- Upfront discounts with high churn or unenforced contracts -> delayed cash and lost terms. Applies to: recurring offers. (47db2b3f88@00:17:31)
- Locked-in lifetime discount when acquisition or delivery cost rises -> each locked customer loses money. Applies to: all. (cd9e0b7e12@00:04:33)
- Free core product with no next paid product -> money lost on free users. Applies to: freemium. (65f6e81dc4@00:03:30)
- Continuity as the only attraction offer when ads need cash -> too little cash today. Applies to: continuity offers. (47db2b3f88@00:01:10)
