## Frameworks
### Seven scaling levels
1. Friends know about it. 2. Consistent outreach and posting. 3. Hire advertisers. 4. Earn referrals. 5. Advertise in more places. 6. Hire department heads. 7. The machine. Each level has one primary action. Use when: scaling from a few million a year to $100M-plus. (fcab34dee8@00:15:38)

### Hiring stages
1. Uncontacted to contacted. 2. Contacted to engaged. 3. Engaged to interviewed. 4. Interviewed to hired. The job offer replaces the sales offer. Use when: general. (71cc0aa386@00:09:53)

### 3D training
1. Document the steps as a checklist. 2. Demonstrate it. 3. Have the hire duplicate it until the result is right. Use when: general. (71cc0aa386@00:12:17)

### Four causes of a performance drop
1. Communication: have the employee describe their job and priorities back to you (71cc0aa386@00:17:46).
2. Training: have them demonstrate, do not ask if they understand; give fast feedback (71cc0aa386@00:18:42).
3. Motivation: check reward timing, frequency, intensity and source; dislike of work; outside events (71cc0aa386@00:19:06).
4. Circumstances: check for real obstacles the owner should have prevented (71cc0aa386@00:20:38).
Use when: performance drops; diagnose in this order. (71cc0aa386@00:16:50)

### Maker and manager day
1. Makers need half-day or full-day blocks. 2. Managers work in 30 to 60 minute blocks. 3. Split the day: maker work first, meetings after 1 p.m. Use when: an entrepreneur does both kinds of work. (5510b30e9a@00:09:16) (5510b30e9a@00:01:24)

### Three CFA levers
1. Lower CAC. 2. Raise lifetime gross profit. 3. Shorten payback period. Use when: general. (7c46bcbf1a@00:00:36)
